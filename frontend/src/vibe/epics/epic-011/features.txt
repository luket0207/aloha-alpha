### Midpoint Alignment

When the vertical centre of the Testimonials section aligns with the vertical centre of the viewport, the background image must be in its neutral, vertically centred position.

### Scroll Behaviour

As the user scrolls down:

1. The initially visible region favours the lower portion of the image.
2. The image moves smoothly upwards.
3. The image reaches its neutral alignment at the section/viewport midpoint.
4. Later scrolling reveals more of the upper portion.

Requirements:

4. Use the Testimonials section's actual rendered dimensions.

## 3. Preserve Safe Background Coverage

The Testimonials section requires sufficient background image overscan for parallax.

Preserve the existing mobile sizing requirement from Epic-010:

- Cover the entire section width.
- Cover the entire visible section height.
- Provide an additional 25% of section height above and below where required.

However, the image should not be enlarged unnecessarily.

1. First calculate the image's proportional height when scaled to the section width.
2. If that height provides sufficient coverage and overscan, use it.
3. Otherwise scale proportionally until the vertical coverage requirement is satisfied.
4. Centre the image horizontally.
5. Centre it vertically at the neutral parallax position.
