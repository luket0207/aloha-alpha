# Epic-003 — Bugs

## Bug-001 — Improve Hero Water Animation

**Status:** complete

### Issue

The animated gradient currently used in the homepage Hero's Light Blue area is far too subtle. The movement is barely noticeable.

I want to replace or significantly improve the animation to create a more recognisable **wavy water effect**.

### Requirements

1. Replace or improve the existing animated gradient with an effect that resembles gently moving water.
2. The animation should contain visible flowing, rippling or undulating movement rather than simply shifting gradient colours.
3. The movement should be noticeably more visible than the current implementation, while remaining subtle enough not to distract from the Hero's content.
4. Retain Light Blue (`#93B7BE`) as the main colour.
5. Use slightly lighter shades of the same blue to create movement, depth and variation.
6. The animation should loop smoothly without obvious jumps or resets.
7. Ensure the effect covers the entire Light Blue area of the Hero.
8. The animation must remain behind the logo and slogan without affecting their visibility.
9. Preserve the existing Hero wave shapes, slideshow, positioning and responsive layout.
10. Ensure the effect works consistently on desktop and mobile.

### Implementation approach

I am happy for you to choose the most appropriate implementation.

Consider techniques such as layered animated gradients, SVG filters, SVG wave patterns or other lightweight CSS/SVG approaches that can produce natural-looking water movement.

Prioritise visual quality, smoothness and performance.

Avoid unnecessarily expensive JavaScript animations or large dependencies.

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by displaying a suitable static water-inspired background when animation is disabled.

### Acceptance criteria

- The animation is clearly visible without being overwhelming.
- The background resembles gently moving water rather than a standard animated gradient.
- The Light Blue colour scheme is preserved.
- The animation is smooth and continuous.
- The logo, slogan and slideshow remain unaffected.
- No significant performance issues are introduced.
- Mobile responsiveness is preserved.

**Notes:** Replaced the single shifting radial gradient with two deterministic, counter-moving SVG ripple layers over the existing Light Blue base. The full Hero water shape now has visibly flowing wave bands on desktop and mobile, while reduced-motion mode retains the layered water texture without animation. Live checks at 320, 375, 768, 1024 and 1440px confirmed full surface coverage, content stacking, smooth movement and no horizontal overflow.

## Bug-002 — Contact Form Wave, Spacing and Layout

**Status:** complete

### Issue

The Contact Us section is functioning correctly, but several layout issues need addressing.

There is a visible white gap between the bottom of the Green Contact Us section and the Light Blue Footer wave in certain places.

Additionally, the Contact Us section is too vertically tall, particularly on desktop.

I want to make the section more compact while retaining its existing functionality and responsive behaviour.

### 1. Fix the Footer Wave Transition

The existing Footer wave should overlap the bottom of the Green Contact Us section without exposing any white background between them.

Requirements:

1. Remove all visible white gaps between the Contact Us section and Footer wave.
2. Ensure the Green background continues underneath the wave wherever necessary.
3. Retain the existing organic wave shape.
4. The transition should appear seamless across the entire width of the page.
5. Ensure the fix works on desktop, tablet and mobile.
6. Avoid introducing horizontal overflow, awkward clipping or visible seams.
7. Do not allow the wave to overlap any contact form fields, buttons or messages.

Inspect the existing wave positioning, background layers, stacking order and section boundaries to identify the cause.

Fix the underlying layout issue rather than hiding the gap using arbitrary fixed offsets that could break at other screen sizes.

### 2. Reduce Contact Section Padding

Reduce the current outer Contact Us section padding as follows:

- **Top padding:** Reduce by 25% (retain 75% of its current value).
- **Bottom padding:** Reduce by 50% (retain 50% of its current value).

Apply these reductions relative to the existing padding values.

Ensure the Footer wave still has sufficient space to overlap the bottom of the section without covering the form.

Do not unnecessarily reduce the internal spacing between individual fields.

### 3. Make the Form More Compact on Desktop

The current form is vertically too tall.

I would like the form to fit within the visible height of most typical desktop screens, where practical, without requiring the user to scroll.

Make the following changes:

**Name field**

- Retain the Name field at full width.

**Email and Phone Number fields**

- Display Email and Phone Number next to each other on the same row at desktop widths.
- Each field should occupy approximately half of the available form width.
- Maintain appropriate spacing between the fields.
- On narrower screens, return to vertically stacked fields.

**Preset Question Buttons**

- Display the three preset question buttons horizontally on desktop rather than vertically.
- Distribute the buttons evenly across the available form width.
- Allow longer Japanese question text to wrap naturally within each button.
- Keep the buttons visually consistent in height where practical.
- On smaller screens, stack the buttons vertically or use a responsive arrangement that avoids cramped text.

**Other spacing**

- Review excessive vertical margins and gaps within the form.
- Reduce unnecessary spacing while keeping the form comfortable to use.
- Do not make input fields or buttons too small.
- Preserve accessibility, focus states and readable Japanese typography.

The objective is a noticeably more compact desktop form, without sacrificing usability or clipping content on smaller or shorter screens.

### 4. Add a Clear Message Button

Add a new button directly beneath the three preset question buttons.

**Button text:**

`入力内容をクリア`

(English: Clear entered content)

Requirements:

1. Clicking this button must empty the message textarea.
2. It must work whether the textarea contains a selected preset question or manually entered text.
3. It must not clear the Name, Email or Phone Number fields.
4. It must not submit the form.
5. The message character counter should reset to zero.
6. Any message-specific validation state should update appropriately.
7. The button must be keyboard accessible.
8. Keep the styling relatively simple, consistent with the existing question buttons.

The Clear button should remain visually distinct from the main Submit button to prevent confusion.

### 5. Preserve Existing Functionality

The following must remain unchanged:

- Japanese form labels and text.
- Required Name, Email and Message fields.
- Optional Phone Number field.
- WordPress-managed preset questions.
- Preset buttons populating the textarea.
- The 1,000-character textarea limit.
- Form validation and error messages.
- WordPress REST API submission.
- FluentSMTP email handling.
- Existing spam and security protections.
- Green Group colour scheme.
- Responsive Contact Us — Jump component.

### Acceptance criteria

- No white gaps appear between Contact Us and the Footer wave.
- The wave transition looks seamless at all supported screen sizes.
- Top padding is reduced by 25%.
- Bottom padding is reduced by 50%.
- Name remains full width.
- Email and Phone Number sit side by side on desktop.
- Preset question buttons are horizontally arranged on desktop.
- Mobile fields and buttons stack appropriately.
- The Clear button empties only the message textarea.
- Character count updates correctly.
- The form is noticeably shorter on desktop.
- Existing submission and validation functionality remains intact.
- No horizontal overflow or content clipping is introduced.

**Notes:** Removed the positive Contact-to-Footer margin that exposed the page background and matched the contact-only Footer wave height to the reduced bottom padding, producing a seamless Green-to-Light-Blue overlap without covering the form. Reduced the section padding to 75% top and 50% bottom, compacted excess form spacing, placed Email/Phone and preset questions into responsive desktop rows, and added an accessible message-only clear button. Build and live checks passed at 320, 375, 768, 1024 and 1440px with no overflow; clear-button state, character count, validation reset and preserved identity fields were verified.
